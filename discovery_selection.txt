**WHERE THE 297 CAME FROM.** A union of four separate selections, each by a different objective, across
several days of analysis: BOOK-50 by decorrelation; a 60-signal set chance-priced against 4,652 nulls;
OPTION-B as a gated production book; S0-120 by seed-0 decorrelation. 350 memberships, 299 distinct, minus two
F1 sequential pairs dropped on measurement = **297, all F0 triples.**

**THERE IS NO SINGLE REPRODUCIBLE PROCEDURE THAT PRODUCES IT.** That is the problem this stage exists to
solve.

**SIX MEASURED FACTS THAT CONSTRAIN THE DESIGN:**

**F1. RANKING SIGNALS DOES NOT WORK HERE.** A rebuilt selector that ranked members **lost to 12 of 12 random
draws** on a holdout. Its objective — minimising the union of losing bars — selected for signals that lose
*together*.

**F2. AND NO PER-SIGNAL METRIC DISCRIMINATES.** 297 leave-one-out runs: `d_net` flat at a 3.4x IQR (p25
$531.60, p75 $1,798.90), 12 of 297 negative, **split-half rho = -0.060 at p = 0.305**, `d_events` moves by
zero for 212 of 297, four source objectives differing by 1.7x, and a 50-draw ablation finding no hidden
collective structure. **The book is homogeneous — nothing to prune and nothing to rank.**

**F3. AND THERE IS A STRUCTURAL REASON.** 121 of 973 entry bars sit at depth **exactly 3**, so removing any
member of such a bar kills every trade on it. **A member's contribution is largely how often it is the
marginal third signal.** Six BOOK-50 members earned $2,649 directly and cost $3,923 to remove.

**F4. THE SCREEN IS THE ONLY VALIDATED COMPONENT — BUT IT HAS BEEN RUN WRONG, AND SECTION 1 CHANGES IT.** The
funnel **as it exists today** is 19,754 raw F0 rows -> 6,488 (full-sample `agg_pf >= 2` AND `folds_plus >= 4`)
-> 6,034 (`trades >= 12`) -> 4,575 by the train-only screen. **253 of 299 adopted members cleared it when the
selection layer was independently rebuilt (84.6%).**

**F5. SIZE IS NOT FREE — THIS KILLS THE OBVIOUS DESIGN.** *(Measured on the 4,575 pool, i.e. WITH the
pre-filter still applied. The corrected stage produces a larger pool, so these are PRE-CORRECTION figures —
the direction holds and holds harder.)* All 4,575 survivors under the adopted architecture: WR 96.12% ->
85.83%, PF 14.53 -> 1.81, **LOSS EVENTS 42 -> 1,113**, worst day -$347 -> **-$7,502**, MARGIN 33.07 -> 8.81.
**AND THE MECHANISM IS THE ARCHITECTURE TURNING AGAINST ITSELF.** At 4,575 signals **82.6% of entry bars
reach depth 5+ with a median depth of 8**, and depth-exactly-3 falls from 12.4% to 3.3%. `tier = min(depth,5)`
routes 82.6% of bars into the d5+ cell, gated by `FailedBreak > p20` — **an 80.19%-pass gate** — and
**`Micro_Hurst > p90` AT BOTH d3 CELLS GOES INERT.** The only two gates in this project ever to clear a null,
at p = 0.000 and p = 0.022, stop firing because almost nothing lands at depth 3. **"SAME ARCHITECTURE" AND

    trades >= 12          TRAIN-WINDOW statistics only
    agg_pf >= 2.0         TRAIN-WINDOW statistics only

**A PROPORTION, NOT A COUNT** — `folds_plus >= 4` as a count is unsatisfiable for 503 signals lacking four

**SCREEN THE RAW 19,754 F0 ROWS DIRECTLY. DO NOT APPLY THE EXISTING 6,488 / 6,034 PRE-FILTER.**

That pre-filter uses full-sample `agg_pf`, `folds_plus` and `trades`, computed over all six monthly folds
**including the months the screen is meant to be validated against.** Layering a train-only screen on top of
it means the population was already selected with the answer. **A REVIEWER CHECKING THE "TRAIN ONLY" CLAIM
WILL FIND IT FALSE IN THE FIRST PLACE THEY LOOK.**

**AND THE EXCLUSION IS NOT SMALL — IT WAS MEASURED.** 300 seeded random signals from the 13,720 the
pre-filter cuts, solo-run and re-screened on train-window data: **37 of 300 pass the full screen.** Projected,
**roughly 1,692 signals the train-only screen would admit are cut before it runs — the eligible population is
understated by about 37%.** And for a monthly stage it compounds: each month the pre-filter consumes the
newest data, so the screen always operates on a population chosen with knowledge of the window it is supposed
to validate against. **THAT DEFEATS THE PURPOSE OF THE STAGE SPECIFICALLY.**

**AND YOU CANNOT SHORTCUT THIS FROM THE ARTIFACT.** `results_F0_*.csv` carries exactly fourteen columns —
`family · script · signal_def · direction · d2d_mode · trades · WR · agg_pf · worst_day_usd · hard_stop_days ·
folds_plus · min_fold_pf · spread_pf · survival` — **all full-sample, with no per-fold net, no per-month
partition and no trade list.** `folds_plus` is a count that does not identify *which* folds were profitable,
`min_fold_pf` is a minimum, and `spread_pf` is a string like `3.17->2.91`. **ALL FOUR SCREEN CRITERIA NEED A
TRAIN-WINDOW PARTITION AND NONE CAN BE RECOVERED FROM THESE COLUMNS.**

**SO THE TRAIN-WINDOW STATISTICS MUST BE COMPUTED, NOT DERIVED. TWO ROUTES:**

**ROUTE A — solo re-run** of all 19,754 rows on train-window data. Roughly **62 minutes at 19,754 x 0.19s**,
parallelisable. **This cost recurs on EVERY monthly run.**

**ROUTE B — emit the partition at scan time.** Modify S3 to write per-month net per signal. **The 62 minutes
disappears on every subsequent run and the screen becomes a table lookup.**

**PREFER ROUTE B IF S3 IS BEING TOUCHED FOR SECTION 4 ANYWAY — WHICH IT LIKELY IS.** Route A costs 62 minutes
every month forever; route B costs it once. **STATE WHICH ROUTE YOU TOOK AND WHY.**

**ANY APPROXIMATION BUILT FROM `folds_plus` OR `min_fold_pf` WILL SILENTLY PRODUCE A DIFFERENT SCREEN, AND
THE SURVIVOR COUNT WILL LOOK PLAUSIBLE WHILE BEING WRONG** — the same failure shape as section 7's passing
verdict. **THE COMPUTATION IS MANDATORY, NOT AN IMPLEMENTATION CHOICE.**

**EXPECT ROUGHLY 6,300 SURVIVORS. ANYWHERE IN 5,800-6,800 IS CONSISTENT WITH THE 300-SIGNAL SAMPLE.**
A count outside that interval means something else is wrong: **under 5,800** suggests the pre-filter is partly
still applied; **over 6,800** suggests the train window is wider than intended or the fold criterion is being
read as a count again; **near 4,575** means the pre-filter has not been removed at all.

**DEFINE TRAIN AS A RULE, NOT A DATE:** "all but the final N months", N from the config. **A hardcoded date
silently changes the screen's meaning every month the stage is run.** State the rule and the resulting window
in the output.

SECTION 2 — SIZE IS A CONFIG PARAMETER, NOT A RANKING. THE TWO ARE SEPARABLE.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Facts F1-F3 rule out ranking members. **They do not rule out controlling size.** **A SEEDED DRAW IS A SIZE
PARAMETER, NOT A RANKING.**

**THE DRAW MUST BE STABLY ORDERED OR THE SEED IS COSMETIC.** `rng.choice(survivors, N)` is reproducible only
if `survivors` is in a stable order — a `set`, a `dict`, a `groupby` on parallel results or concatenated
worker output can all reorder, and **the same seed then selects different signals at different worker
counts.** **SORT BY A STABLE KEY — `signal_def` + `direction`, NEVER A FLOAT — AND ASSERT THE SURVIVOR LIST
IS IDENTICAL ACROSS WORKER COUNTS BEFORE DRAWING.** Otherwise determinism is satisfied in appearance and
violated in fact, which is the same shape as three defects this project has already had.

**THE SIX ARMS MUST BE NESTED, NOT SIX INDEPENDENT DRAWS.** Draw-to-draw variation is large — 21 to 27 events
at N=150, 35 to 49 at N=200 — so **independent draws confound size with draw luck and the sweep becomes
unreadable.**

    survivors = sorted(survivors, key=lambda s: (s.signal_def, s.direction))   # stable, never a float
    rng   = np.random.default_rng(SEED)
    order = rng.permutation(len(survivors))          # ONE permutation, recorded with SEED
    arms  = {n: sorted(order[:n]) for n in (150, 200, 297, 500, 1000, len(survivors))}
    # NOTE: arms holds INDICES. the book for arm n is [survivors[i] for i in arms[n]]

`order[:150]` is a strict subset of `order[:200]` by construction, and `sorted()` gives a stable within-arm
order for the CSV. **SIZE BECOMES THE ONLY THING THAT VARIES BETWEEN ARMS.**

**`book_size` NAMES WHICH ARM IS WRITTEN AS THE BOOK. ALL SIX ARE SCORED; ONLY ONE IS EMITTED AS
`<name>_signals.csv`.** The other five appear in the scorecard and side-by-side only. `<name>_selection.csv`
and `<name>_rejected.csv` **describe the `book_size` arm**, and every row carries `SEED` and `book_size` so
the emitted book is reproducible from the permutation alone. **IF `book_size` IS NOT ONE OF THE SIX EMITTED
SIZES, ABORT** — do not silently draw a seventh arm and do not round to the nearest.

**DO NOT TIGHTEN THE SCREEN TO HIT A TARGET SIZE.** A threshold moved to reach a number is a fitted threshold
on the same frame.

**EMIT THE SCORE AT SIX SIZES: 150, 200, 297, 500, 1,000, AND ALL SURVIVORS.**

**THE SWEEP MUST GO BELOW 297, BECAUSE THAT IS WHERE THE ANSWER IS.** Loss events scale roughly with book
size, so every arm at or above 297 is on the losing half of the curve. **N=150 gives 21-27 events against the
incumbent's 42, with margin 27.2-28.5 — A RANDOM DRAW AT 150 BEATS THE INCUMBENT ON LOSS EVENTS.**

**AND IT LOSES ON PARTICIPATION, WHICH IS WHY BOTH ENDS MUST BE PRINTED:** 84-90 days traded at N=150 against
the incumbent's 119. **The operator rules on that trade-off and cannot see it if only the upward half is
emitted.**

**AND NO DRAW AT ANY SIZE HELD 26 CLEAN WEEKS** — 1 to 3 losing weeks at 150, 200, 297, 500 and 1,000 alike.
**DO NOT REPORT A LOSING WEEK AS A SIZE PROBLEM; IT IS PRESENT THROUGHOUT.**

**RUNTIME: the all-survivors pass was ~165s on 4,575 signals against ~2s at 297. At ~6,300 that is ~226s by
linear scaling, and engine cost was SUPERLINEAR in book size in every measurement — TREAT ~226s AS A FLOOR.**
A long stage is not a hang.
