THE ELIGIBILITY LAYER — THE FOUR YOU SAID HAD NO DERIVATION
ADX_Value >= 15 — it has a mechanism, and the mechanism is structural

Mechanism, stated before the numbers: the threshold oracle's rolling 2,500-bar ring only admits bars where ADX >= 15 AND Volume > 50 (dots_thresholds.compute_adaptive_thresholds). Every hi/lo percentile in all 299 signals is therefore calibrated on the ADX≥15 population. Entering on a bar outside that population means firing thresholds against a distribution they were never computed from. ADX ≥ 15 is the self-consistency point between the oracle and the entry gate. That is why 15 and not 14 or 16 — it is not a tuned volatility filter, it is the same number twice.

WHAT I'D ACT ON

Two constants are not doing anything and can be said so plainly: Volume > 50 and Micro_FailedBreak > p20 at LONG d4. One framing in the existing spec should be reversed: RISK_MULT / MAX_RISK / STEP_PCT are spikes. "All alternatives worse" reads as validation and means the opposite. And the best-evidenced thing in your system is the one you built it around. The depth floor. Cliff below, plateau above, confirmed independently in both directions, sixteen cells deep. Concurrence earns its place on the measurement, not on the story.
