 4. HOW THE SET WAS BUILT — REPRODUCIBLE PROCEDURE
================================================================================

  STEP 1  Score all 1,818 VALID F0 catalogue signals for per-signal DAILY P&L.
          Run in batches of 120 through portfolio_simulation_engine.py so the
          6-lot jar behaves as it would in a real book. 9 seconds, 121,966 trades.

  STEP 2  Build nine DIVERSE selections of 50 by greedy decorrelation over that
          field, varying the objective (min shared loss-days / max net per overlap /
          min P&L correlation) and the seed (best ratio / max net / fewest losses).
          Union them with BOOK-50's 48 F0 signals -> a 231-signal candidate field.

  STEP 3  Re-decorrelate the union: greedy admission on MINIMUM SHARED LOSS DAYS,
          net-positive signals only, PER DIRECTION SEPARATELY, to 70L/50S.

  STEP 4  Find the gate per (direction x depth-tier) cell by coordinate descent
          over ~150 gate options (16 variables x 8 percentiles x hi/lo, plus tick
          and ADX thresholds), objective = maximise PF subject to a net floor and
          a worst-day floor. Four passes to convergence.

  WHY EACH STEP IS THERE — the five findings from seven attempts:
   1. THE DECORRELATION FIELD SIZE IS THE BINDING CONSTRAINT. 300 pre-filtered
      candidates gave win/loss 0.70; all 1,818 gave 0.98.
   2. RANK ON RARITY, NOT PERSISTENCE. folds_plus ranking gave WORSE depth quality
      than EXPECTED_ROWS_AT_OR_ABOVE_THIS_PF. Persistent signals fire often and
      stack badly.
   3. THE FREE-TIER DEPTH MOVES WITH BOOK SIZE. 48 signals cross at depth 3; 120
      cross at 4-6. Inheriting "triple+ free" cost $1,522 in a single day.
   4. GATED SHALLOW TRADES IMPROVE THE TAIL. They lose on different days from the
      triples. A triples-only book is NOT the optimum.
   5. METHOD DIVERSITY FUSES; BOOK COUNT DOES NOT. BOOK-50 fused with one
      independently-selected 50 (3 of 97 overlapping) beat both parents. Nine
      greedy variants of the same field added almost nothing.

  THE THREE LOSING DAYS SURVIVE EVERY SETTING — the gate does not touch them.

  WHY IT WORKS — the mechanism, measured:

    band              trades    net     BE exits   avg BE   SL   median ATR  spread as
                                                                              % of the
                                                                              BE trigger
    LOW  ATR <25.8       391   $7,097      251     $10.92    32     20.6        24%
    HIGHER               1,597  $92,997    1,264   $34.11    31     46.7        11%

  At low ATR the break-even lock sits ~12 points out and the 3.0 spread eats a QUARTER
  of it, so a BE exit banks $10.92 against $34.11 in normal conditions. The low band
  still nets +$7,097 — it does not bleed, it earns $18.15 a trade against $58.23 — but
  it carries a disproportionate share of the stop-outs for very little return.

  WHAT WAS TRIED AND REJECTED:

    MIN_BE_TRIGGER (a floor on the break-even distance, in points) — FAILS.
      floor 10 -> losses 68 -> 74, PF 14.14 -> 13.88
      floor 25 -> losses 68 -> 108, PF 14.14 -> 11.08
    Widening the trigger means FEWER trades reach break-even at all, so more ride to the
    stop. The BE mechanism's value is HOW OFTEN IT FIRES, not how much it banks. Every
    BE-floor + ATR-gate combination underperformed the ATR gate alone.

    A MINIMUM STOP (floor on ATR feeding the risk calculation) — FAILS.
      Minimum ATR_1M in the frame is 8.5, so floors below 15 do not bind at all, and
      floors at 20+ make it worse (PF 8.53 -> 7.53 at ATR floor 25) because flooring ATR
      widens the STOP and the STEP together, pushing the BE trigger further away.

    DO NOT MOVE THE TRIGGER. DO NOT TAKE THE TRADE.

  ALSO SWEPT AND REJECTED — the full trade-management constant set:

    RISK_MULT 1.5 / 2.5 / 3.0 · MOMENTUM_SL_MULT 3 / 5 / 6 · STEP_PCT 0.20 / 0.25 / 0.40
    / 0.50 · LAG_BASE 1 / 3 · LAG_MOMENTUM 2 / 4 / 5 · MOMENTUM_THRESHOLD 6e-5 / 2e-4 /
    3e-4 · MAX_RISK 120 / 200 / 300 · BE_TRIG_FRAC 0.7 / 1.5 · LOCK_FRAC 0.5 / 1.5

  NOTHING BEAT THE RATIFIED VALUES. The only marginal gain was LAG_BASE=3 at +0.33 PF
  and no cost — within noise. THE TRADE MANAGEMENT IS ALREADY TUNED.

  AND ONE TRAP FOUND WHILE SWEEPING, WHICH MUST NOT BE REPEATED:

    LOCK_FRAC   net        exits at prices NEVER TRADED
      1.0    $158,418        0  (0.0%)      <- the ratified value, the CEILING
      1.5    $195,850    2,045 (31.1%)
      2.0    $258,236    3,503 (52.7%)
      3.0    $413,696    5,108 (76.4%)

  Break-even fires when price reaches entry + be_trigger. At LOCK_FRAC 1.0 the stop moves
  to exactly that price — reachable, because price just got there. ABOVE 1.0 THE STOP IS

  same shape as the recorded defect that once took PF 1.65 to 1.01.

 8.1  THE GAP-FILLER SYSTEM MUST NOT BE APPLIED TO OPTION B.

      Option B, NO gaps     1,988 tr  $100,094  WR 96.58%  PF 14.14  wd -$272.9   68 losses  3 losing days
      Option B + gaps       2,546 tr  $116,028  WR 94.70%  PF  8.06  wd -$495.6  135 losses  7 losing days

      +$16K for DOUBLE the losses, double the losing days, PF nearly halved and an 82% worse
      tail. And the damage is not the gaps' own P&L — the BOOK degrades before a single gap
      trade is counted (1,988 -> 2,323 trades, $100,094 -> $103,464, wd -$272.9 -> -$495.6),
      because a gap holds a lot in the 6-lot jar and changes which book trades are admitted
      afterwards. Option B's gates leave the book flat 80.5% of the time, so gaps fire into
      space the book was about to use. On BOOK-50 the gaps IMPROVE the worst day (-153.7 ->
      -104.4); that behaviour does NOT transfer to a more heavily gated book.
      ADOPTED CONFIGURATION: OPTION B WITHOUT GAP FILLERS.

 8.6  THREE CORRECTIONS TO THE FIRST DRAFT.

      (a) THE WALK-FORWARD IN SECTION 6 TESTED A DIFFERENT GATE SET. Those gates were fitted
          on Jan-May and cover 1,146 trades. The headline system's gates were fitted on all
          seven months and cover 1,988. Section 6 demonstrates that a coordinate-descent gate
          fit GENERALISES. It does NOT put the headline artifact out of sample. The headline
          gate set has never been tested on unseen data.
      (b) THE REAL BUILD ITEM IS JAR ADMISSION ORDER, NOT DEPTH COUNTING. The EA already
          evaluates every rule every bar, so the depth tally is a by-product of that loop and
          costs nothing. What is unspecified: when a depth-5 bar generates more qualifying
          entries than six live lots can hold, WHICH FIVE GET IN. Today it is first-come within
          the loop. Under depth gating that becomes a policy decision nobody has made.
      (c) THE ONE F1 SIGNAL CAME FROM BOOK-50, NOT FROM CATALOGUE-SHOPPING.
          `Sqz_Val:hi ->13-> Micro_OrderFlowDelta:lo LONG` is BOOK-50's SQUEEZE_BREAKOUT
          structure-fill. The fusion step unioned BOOK-50 in, and decorrelation kept it on
          merit. The F0-only rule governed selection FROM THE CATALOGUE and was not broken;
          the incumbent's own signal earned its place in a fused book.
