0. WHAT WOULD MAKE THIS SPECIFICATION WRONG
================================================================================

Two systems in two days have had a structural defect found by the next reader.
Not a rounding error — a defect that changed what the system was. Expect a
third. This section exists to help you find it faster than the last two were
found.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0.1  THE TWO DEFECTS ALREADY FOUND, AND WHAT THEY LOOKED LIKE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

DEFECT 1 — THE JAR WAS SILENTLY ACTING AS A GATE.
  Executed depth is capped by free jar slots, so a 9-signal event squeezed into
  4 slots was recorded as depth 4 and received the d4 gate instead of the d5+
  gate. Every re-tiering is DOWNWARD, so the gate applied was always STRICTER
  than the market state warranted. Measured: 446 of 2,019 admitted bars
  re-tiered, and they carried a 1.66% loss rate against 3.95% for correctly
  tiered bars. The book looked 35 loss events safer than it was.
  IT LOOKED LIKE: a number that was better than its own mechanism could explain.

DEFECT 2 — THE DEPTH FLOOR WAS A POST-HOC FILTER, NOT AN ADMISSION RULE.
  The engine admitted signals one at a time until the jar was full, then a
  filter deleted every trade whose bar-depth was below 3. In a simulator those
  trades vanish. In a live engine they are simply open. 9,356 trades were
  deleted this way, of which 2,068 belonged to events that were valid in market
  terms (qualification depth >= 3) and were chopped only by where the cap
  happened to land. A live EA built to v1 would have run a book nobody scored.
  IT LOOKED LIKE: a rule stated in the spec that no engine could execute at
  decision time.

BOTH SHARE A SHAPE: the specification described a quantity the engine computed
differently. That is the shape to hunt for.

    current_sl at that bar. Any systematic negative difference re-prices the
    ceiling as (at-risk x MAX_RISK x conviction x lot) + (locked x gap-through

    measurement. See section 8.4.

0.4  WHAT IS TESTED AND WHAT IS NOT — PLAINLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

TESTED
  Signal selection required 4-of-6-month consistency. Every one of the 299 had
  to clear agg_pf >= 2.0 AND folds_plus >= 4 over six separate monthly folds.
  A signal that worked in one month only fails that. This is a STABILITY test,
  not a holdout — all six folds sit inside the same six months.

  July is a genuine holdout for the signal layer. The folds cover 2026.01 to
  2026.06; 19,842 bars of July were in no fold and played no part in selecting
  any signal. July is the strongest month in the book: 419 trades, WR 97.14%,
  PF 41.35, worst day -$16.8. BASE: 13 days and 3 LOSS EVENTS. Three events
  cannot carry an interval and this must not be quoted as proof.

  Seven of seven months positive. Twenty-six of twenty-six weeks positive.

  All 60 compositions in the walk-forward universe are profitable in every
  test window. Worst configuration, weakest window: $8,102.

NOT TESTED
  The composition layer — floors, tier gates, cap, admission rule — was chosen
  with the whole frame visible. It has no holdout.

  This exact configuration has never been run forward on unseen data.

  The gate at LONG d5+ has no null (R3).

  The admission order has never been varied (R2).

THE FINDING THAT MATTERS MOST
  Across 60 complete compositions and three walk-forward windows, the spread
  between the best and worst composition is roughly 25-30% of net, and every
  one of them is profitable in every window. The composition choice moves you
  around inside a band. It does not decide whether the system works.
  THE SIGNALS CARRY IT. THE COMPOSITION IS A PREFERENCE.

2. THE RULE, IN EXECUTION ORDER
================================================================================

STEP 1 — BAR ELIGIBILITY
  bar >= 6900                    Dots_InitBars warmup floor. Ring saturation is
                                 bar 5,204, so the floor binds. First scannable
                                 bar 2026.01.26 20:54.
  ADX_Value >= 15
  Volume > 50
  Volume != 0
  NOT (EST_DayOfWeek == 5 AND (EST_Hour > 16 OR (EST_Hour == 16 AND
       EST_Minute >= 45)))       Friday cutoff.

STEP 2 — SIGNAL QUALIFICATION
  299 signals, listed in full in section 4.

  297 are F0 triple-convergence: three conditions ANDed, each of the form
  VARIABLE:hi, VARIABLE:lo or VARIABLE:==N. hi/lo are mechanism-D adaptive
  percentiles from dots_thresholds.py (rolling 2,500 eligible bars,
  day-refreshed, floor-index), hi = p80 and lo = p20, or the two structural
  constants (VWAP_Z +/-2; OR_Position 0.80/0.20, gated on a valid OR set).

  TWO — NOT ONE — ARE F1 SEQUENTIAL PAIRS of the form A ->k-> B, evaluated by
  sequential_temporal.pair_mask with the ST_Flip anchor (ST_Flip_Event != 0):
      LONG   1   ADX_Rising:==0 ->8-> D2D_DirStep:==-1
      LONG 182   Sqz_Val:hi ->13-> Micro_OrderFlowDelta:lo
  v1 said one. A build that reads v1 literally drops or mis-parses the other,
  and the F1 path needs an anchor the F0 path does not supply.

  MANDATORY FOURTH TERM ON EVERY SIGNAL, BOTH FAMILIES:
      D2D_Trend_Dir == trade direction

STEP 3 — DEPTH.  READ THIS DEFINITION CAREFULLY. IT IS NOT v1's.

  DEPTH IS THE NUMBER OF SIGNALS ADMITTED IN THE BATCH AT THAT BAR IN THAT
  DIRECTION. It is decided by STEP 4 below, before any position is opened, and
  it is knowable at bar close.

  It is NOT the count of signals that qualify. Qualification depth ignores the
  jar and ignores signals already holding a position; it is a different, worse
  system (6,226 trades / 118 loss events / PF 8.63 under v1's other settings).

  It is also NOT v1's executed depth, which was only knowable AFTER a bar's
  admissions finished and therefore could not be evaluated at decision time.

STEP 4 — ADMISSION (FLOORED).  THE WHOLE BAR IS DECIDED AS ONE BATCH.

  At bar close, compute once:
      live   = number of active trades that are NOT be_nudged
      free   = MAX_POSITIONS - live                                  (16 - live)

  Then, for each direction d in (LONG, SHORT) in that order:
      A = signals of direction d that qualify at this bar AND are not already
          holding a position
      if len(A) == 0                       -> nothing for this direction
      if free < 3                          -> admit nothing, this direction
      n = min(len(A), free)
      if n < 3                             -> admit nothing, this direction
      evaluate STEP 5 and STEP 6 at depth n
      if they pass                         -> ADMIT ALL n, set free = free - n
      else                                 -> admit nothing, this direction

  NO POSITION IS EVER OPENED AND THEN REMOVED. There is no post-hoc filter.
  Backtest equals live, exactly.

  ADMISSION ORDER IS LONG THEN SHORT. This is a live parameter, it was
  inherited, and it has never been varied. See section 0.3 R2.

STEP 5 — GLOBAL GATE
  ATR_1M >= 20 at the entry bar. Raw ATR, not a percentile.

STEP 6 — PER-TIER GATE STACK.  tier = min(depth, 5).

  tier   LONG                                              SHORT
  ----   ----------------------------------------------    -------------------
  1      unreachable (floor is 3)                          unreachable
  2      unreachable (floor is 3)                          unreachable
  3      Micro_Hurst > p90                                 Micro_Hurst > p90
  4      Micro_FailedBreak > p20 AND AT_Slope_ST > p90     free
  5+     Micro_FailedBreak > p20                           free

  All four thresholds are mechanism-D adaptive percentiles at the entry bar.
  A build MUST NOT carry the tier-1 and tier-2 cells: they are unreachable
  under a floor of 3 and are not part of this system.

STEP 7 — THE JAR
  live_lots = count of active trades that are NOT be_nudged. Cap 16.
  THE JAR COUNTS AT-RISK POSITIONS, NOT LOTS AND NOT OPEN POSITIONS. A trade
  past its break-even nudge cannot lose and frees its slot. This is why peak
  OPEN reaches 43 while peak AT-RISK is exactly 16.

STEP 8 — CONVICTION SIZING
  Multiplies lots. Never blocks an entry. These are ASSIGNMENTS, not products,
  so the maximum multiplier is 2.0:
      Micro_Hurst > p90 on longs                          x2.0
      Micro_FailedBreak > p90 within the last 5 bars      x1.25
      D2D agreement (D2D_Signal == dir AND ADX >= 30
         AND Micro_Hurst >= p30), both directions         x2.0

STEP 9 — TRADE MANAGEMENT
  momentum = Micro_LogReturn * direction >= 0.00012
  raw_risk = ATR_1M * (4.0 if momentum else 2.0)
  initial_risk = min(raw_risk, 150.0)          <- 150 POINTS. NOT DOLLARS.
  stop = entry -/+ initial_risk
  base_risk = min(ATR_1M * 2.0, 150.0)
  step_size = 0.30 * base_risk
  be_trigger = 1.00 * step_size
  be_lock_dist = 1.00 * be_trigger
  lag = 3 if momentum else 2
  SPREAD = 3.0 points, charged on every trade
  Entry price is the bar CLOSE. Every trade opened on the same bar in the same
  direction therefore has an IDENTICAL entry, stop, lag and conviction
  multiplier, and differs only in which signal opened it.

Every constant, once, with its derivation and one of four labels.

  DERIVED    a named measurement produced this value
  SWEPT      neighbours tested, curve reported, adopted value defended
  INHERITED  carried from an earlier build, survives on that basis
  ARBITRARY  no mechanism, no sweep, no derivation — and it is said

Where a constant is a GATE, its rarity-matched null is stated. The null is
replace-not-stack: the candidate replaces the adopted gate at that cell, the
rest of the stack is held, and the comparison is loss EVENTS.

8. RISK POSTURE
================================================================================

8.1  THE FAILURE MODE IS A SINGLE BAR, NOT A BAD DAY
  Every loss event is one bar's cluster of 3 to 9 identical positions. The book
  does not bleed; it takes occasional craters. The deepest reconstructed
  intraday moment is -$845.91 and every deep day in the sample closed green.
  THAT IS NOT REASSURANCE — it is 43 observations of a mechanism that has never
  met a day where the recovery did not come.

8.2  THE CAP IS AN EXPOSURE DIAL, NOT A FILTER
  See section 3. Events flat 14->24, net monotone. Under the previous admission
  rule the cap was silently filtering; it is not any more. v1's warning that
  "cap 8 is the worst setting in every configuration and the reason is unknown"
  does not hold here: net is monotone from 6 to 24 and cap 6 is the worst.

8.3  THE THREE SPIKES
  RISK_MULT, MAX_RISK and STEP_PCT are one surface and each sits on a point
  where both neighbours are far worse. If live behaviour degrades without an
  obvious cause, this is the first place to look. See section 3 and 0.3 R8.

8.4  THE CEILING, AND THE ONE ASSUMPTION THE WHOLE EXPOSURE ARGUMENT RESTS ON

  ceiling = MAX_POSITIONS x MAX_RISK x max conviction x lot
          = 16 x 150 x 2.0 x 0.30 = $1,440 = 28.8% of the $5,000 FTMO daily

  That figure counts AT-RISK positions only. Peak OPEN reaches 43 while peak
  AT-RISK is exactly 16, and the difference is legitimate ONLY BECAUSE
  BREAK-EVEN-LOCKED POSITIONS CANNOT LOSE. Section 6.3 establishes that from
  source and from 5,317 measured trades.

  IT IS TRUE OF THE SIMULATOR BY CONSTRUCTION. IT IS TRUE OF LIVE ONLY TO THE
  EXTENT FILLS ARE CLEAN.

  The engine fills at current_sl EXACTLY whenever the bar touches it. There is
  no slippage model and no gap model anywhere in the exit path. A gap through
  the lock fills BELOW the lock, and a locked position would then lose. This
  frame cannot measure how far below, because the fill assumption is what
  produced every exit price in it.

  CONSEQUENCE, STATED WITHOUT SOFTENING:
    - $1,440 is EXACT under the simulator's fill assumption
    - $1,440 is a LOWER BOUND live
    - every intraday figure in 6.4 inherits the same assumption, because the
      marking is floored at each position's own stop
    - the true live ceiling is (at-risk x MAX_RISK x conviction x lot)
      + (locked positions x whatever a gap can take through the lock), and the
      second term is UNMEASURED

  THIS IS WHAT THE DEMO IS FOR. Comparing live fills against modelled fills
  over a demo period is the only way to price the second term, and it is the
  single most valuable measurement available before size is increased.

9. BUILD ITEMS
================================================================================

B1  GAP FILLERS DO NOT SCALE WITH THE BASE LOT.
    portfolio_simulation_engine.py L302 hardcodes glots = 1.0; L306 reads the
    module constant D2D_GAP_LOTS = 2.0. Neither reads long_mult/short_mult.
    At a 0.30 base, GAP_HURST and GAP_FB run at 3.33x intended size.
    They are excluded from every figure in this document, so nothing here is
    affected. A live deployment at 0.30 would carry them at full size.
    DECIDE: scale them with the base lot, or disable them. Do not ship as is.

B2  THE ADMISSION BATCH MUST BE ONE PASS AT BAR CLOSE.
    Compute free once, decide the whole direction group, then open. An
    implementation that opens positions inside the loop and re-reads free will
    reproduce the v1 defect.

B3  TWO F1 SEQUENTIAL SIGNALS NEED THE ST_Flip ANCHOR.
    The F0 path does not supply it. See section 2 STEP 2.

B4  NO POST-HOC FILTER ANYWHERE.
    If any stage of the build deletes a trade after it was opened, the build is
    wrong. Every gate and the floor are evaluated before any position exists.

B5  MACRO ORDERING.
    #define macros must precede first use. Standing check on any cross-section
    move in the EA.

10. WHAT THIS DOCUMENT DOES NOT CLAIM
================================================================================

It does not claim this composition is optimal. Section 7.3 shows it is not
distinguishable from 59 alternatives out-of-sample.

It does not claim the composition has been tested forward. It has not.

It does not claim 43 loss events support a confidence interval on anything
day-keyed; those rest on 36 days.

It does not claim the intraday figures are engine output. They are a
reconstruction with a stated, arguable convention.

It does not claim a second implementation has reproduced any of this. None has.

WHAT IT DOES CLAIM: the signal layer was selected for 4-of-6-month consistency,
the one true holdout available is the strongest month in the book, seven of
seven months and twenty-six of twenty-six weeks are positive, and across sixty
complete compositions and three walk-forward windows every single one is
profitable. THE SIGNALS CARRY IT. THE COMPOSITION IS A PREFERENCE.
