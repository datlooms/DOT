 4. HOW THE SET WAS BUILT — REPRODUCIBLE PROCEDURE
================================================================================

  STEP 1  Score all 1,818 VALID F0 catalogue signals for per-signal DAILY P&L.
          Run in batches of 120 through portfolio_simulation_engine.py so the
          6-lot jar behaves as it would in a real book. 9 seconds, 121,966 trades.

  STEP 2  Build nine DIVERSE selections of 50 by greedy decorrelation over that
          field, varying the objective (min shared loss-days / max net per overlap /
          min P&L correlation) and the seed (best ratio / max net / fewest losses).
          Union them with BOOK-50's 48 F0 signals -> a 231-signal candidate field.

  STEP 3  Re-decorrelate the union: greedy admission on MINIMUM SHARED LOSS DAYS,
          net-positive signals only, PER DIRECTION SEPARATELY, to 70L/50S.

  STEP 4  Find the gate per (direction x depth-tier) cell by coordinate descent
          over ~150 gate options (16 variables x 8 percentiles x hi/lo, plus tick
          and ADX thresholds), objective = maximise PF subject to a net floor and
          a worst-day floor. Four passes to convergence.

  WHY EACH STEP IS THERE — the five findings from seven attempts:
   1. THE DECORRELATION FIELD SIZE IS THE BINDING CONSTRAINT. 300 pre-filtered
      candidates gave win/loss 0.70; all 1,818 gave 0.98.
   2. RANK ON RARITY, NOT PERSISTENCE. folds_plus ranking gave WORSE depth quality
      than EXPECTED_ROWS_AT_OR_ABOVE_THIS_PF. Persistent signals fire often and
      stack badly.
   3. THE FREE-TIER DEPTH MOVES WITH BOOK SIZE. 48 signals cross at depth 3; 120
      cross at 4-6. Inheriting "triple+ free" cost $1,522 in a single day.
   4. GATED SHALLOW TRADES IMPROVE THE TAIL. They lose on different days from the
      triples. A triples-only book is NOT the optimum.
   5. METHOD DIVERSITY FUSES; BOOK COUNT DOES NOT. BOOK-50 fused with one
      independently-selected 50 (3 of 97 overlapping) beat both parents. Nine
      greedy variants of the same field added almost nothing.

  At low ATR the break-even lock sits ~12 points out and the 3.0 spread eats a QUARTER
  of it, so a BE exit banks $10.92 against $34.11 in normal conditions. The low band
  still nets +$7,097 — it does not bleed, it earns $18.15 a trade against $58.23 — but
  it carries a disproportionate share of the stop-outs for very little return.

  WHAT WAS TRIED AND REJECTED:

    MIN_BE_TRIGGER (a floor on the break-even distance, in points) — FAILS.
      floor 10 -> losses 68 -> 74, PF 14.14 -> 13.88
      floor 25 -> losses 68 -> 108, PF 14.14 -> 11.08
    Widening the trigger means FEWER trades reach break-even at all, so more ride to the
    stop. The BE mechanism's value is HOW OFTEN IT FIRES, not how much it banks. Every
    BE-floor + ATR-gate combination underperformed the ATR gate alone.

    A MINIMUM STOP (floor on ATR feeding the risk calculation) — FAILS.
      Minimum ATR_1M in the frame is 8.5, so floors below 15 do not bind at all, and
      floors at 20+ make it worse (PF 8.53 -> 7.53 at ATR floor 25) because flooring ATR
      widens the STOP and the STEP together, pushing the BE trigger further away.

    DO NOT MOVE THE TRIGGER. DO NOT TAKE THE TRADE.

  ALSO SWEPT AND REJECTED — the full trade-management constant set:

 5F. p90 VERSUS p95 ON THE LONG SOLO GATE — A REAL, UNPRICED DECISION
================================================================================

  Isolated by a Quant reproduction on the 120-signal book, everything else identical:

                       trades      WR      PF   losses      net   worst day
    p90 (ADOPTED)       1,785   97.65   16.66       42   $96,876    -$278.4
    p95                 1,749   98.00   23.02       35   $95,986    -$203.1
    delta                 -36  +0.35pp    +38%       -7     -$890   27% BETTER

  **p95 DOMINATES p90 ON EVERY AXIS EXCEPT NET, AND IT COSTS 0.9% OF NET.** A 27% better worst day
  and seven fewer losses for $890. On a survival-first mandate that is a favourable trade, and it
  requires no new gate, no new variable and no signal change — it is one constant already in the
  stack.

  **AND IT IS NOT FREE, FOR ONE REASON THAT MUST TRAVEL WITH IT.** Choosing p95 because it measured
  better here is a search over two settings. Two trials is a trivial multiple-testing burden but it
