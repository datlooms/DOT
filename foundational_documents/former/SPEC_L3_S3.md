## 14. WHAT THIS IS NOT

- **Not out-of-sample.** All headline figures in-sample; the three test windows come from a full-sample-selected union.
- **Not walked forward as a process.** Only 42% of the 299 signals would have been in the field on split-0 training data.
- **The depth floor is a post-hoc filter, not an admission rule.** A real build frees jar slots and trades more.
- **The intraday figures are a reconstruction.** The engine holds zero equity/floating references.
- **`MAX_POSITIONS` 10 or 12 is an engine change.** The current value is 6.
- **Cells below 20 observations carry no rate** and are marked `n<20`.
