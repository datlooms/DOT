0. WHAT WOULD MAKE THIS SPECIFICATION WRONG
================================================================================

Three systems have now had a structural defect found by the next reader. Expect
a fourth. This section exists to make it cheaper to find.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0.1  THE THREE DEFECTS ALREADY FOUND, AND WHAT EACH LOOKED LIKE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

D1  THE JAR WAS SILENTLY ACTING AS A GATE. Executed depth was capped by free
    slots, so a 9-signal event squeezed into 4 was recorded as d4 and received
    the d4 gate instead of the d5+ gate. Every re-tiering is DOWNWARD, so the
    gate applied was always STRICTER than the market state warranted. 446 of
    2,019 admitted bars re-tiered, carrying a 1.66% loss rate against 3.95%.
    The book looked ~35 loss events safer than it was.
    SHAPE: a number better than its own mechanism could explain.

D2  THE DEPTH FLOOR WAS A POST-HOC FILTER, NOT AN ADMISSION RULE. The engine
    admitted one signal at a time until full, then a filter deleted every trade
    whose bar-depth was under 3. In a simulator those vanish; in a live engine
    they are open. 9,356 trades were deleted this way, 2,068 of them belonging
    to events valid in market terms.
    SHAPE: a rule no engine could execute at decision time.

D3  A MECHANISM WAS INVENTED TO EXPLAIN A CORRECT NUMBER. spec_2 §6.3 explained
    four non-SL losses as "a break-even lock net of spread landing fractionally
    negative." The smallest possible lock is 12 points against a 3-point
    spread, so it cannot arise. The number was right; the explanation was
    fabricated. Corrected in 6.3 below from source.
    SHAPE: a plausible story attached to a figure nobody had traced.

0.4  WHAT IS TESTED AND WHAT IS NOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

TESTED
  Signal selection required 4-of-6-month consistency: every one of the 299 had
  to clear agg_pf >= 2.0 AND folds_plus >= 4 across six separate monthly folds.
  This is a STABILITY test, not a holdout — all six folds sit inside the same
  six months.
  July is a genuine holdout for the signal layer: the folds cover 2026.01 to
  2026.06 and July was in none of them. July is the strongest month —
  437 trades, WR 97.25%, PF 40.71. BASE: 3 LOSS EVENTS. It cannot carry an
  interval and must not be quoted as proof.
  Seven of seven months positive. Twenty-six of twenty-six weeks positive.
  An independent rebuild of the selection layer, on train data only with the
  fold criterion as a proportion, retained 253 of the 278 adopted signals that
  are in the F0 field — 84.6% of the book. THE BOOK IS NOT AN ARTEFACT OF THE
  ADMISSION DEFECT.
  All 60 compositions in the walk-forward universe are profitable in every test
  window; worst configuration in the weakest window still makes money.

NOT TESTED
  The composition layer — floors, tier gates, cap — was chosen with the whole
  frame visible. No holdout.
  This exact configuration has never been run forward on unseen data.
  The LONG d5+ gate has no null (R3).
  The fill model has never been compared against live fills (R2).

THE FINDING THAT MATTERS MOST
  Across 60 complete compositions and three walk-forward windows the spread
  between best and worst is 25-30% of net and EVERY ONE IS PROFITABLE. A
  rebuilt selector using an event-based decorrelation objective performed WORSE
  THAN DRAWING AT RANDOM (12 of 12 random draws beat it), and it was reported
  as such. The composition choice moves you inside a band; it does not decide
  whether the system works.
  THE SIGNALS CARRY IT. THE COMPOSITION IS A PREFERENCE.

2. THE RULE, IN EXECUTION ORDER
================================================================================

STEP 1 — BAR ELIGIBILITY
  bar >= 6900          Dots_InitBars. Ring saturates at 5,204, so the floor
                       binds. First scannable bar 2026.01.26 20:54.
  ADX_Value >= 15
  Volume > 50
  Volume != 0
  NOT (EST_DayOfWeek == 5 AND (EST_Hour > 16 OR
       (EST_Hour == 16 AND EST_Minute >= 45)))

STEP 2 — SIGNAL QUALIFICATION
  299 signals, listed in full in section 4.
  297 are F0 triple-convergence: three conditions ANDed, each VARIABLE:hi,
  VARIABLE:lo or VARIABLE:==N. hi/lo are mechanism-D adaptive percentiles from
  dots_thresholds.py (rolling 2,500 eligible bars, day-refreshed, floor-index),
  hi = p80 and lo = p20; or the structural constants (VWAP_Z +/-2;
  OR_Position 0.80/0.20, gated on a valid OR set).

  TWO — NOT ONE — ARE F1 SEQUENTIAL PAIRS of form A ->k-> B, evaluated by
  sequential_temporal.pair_mask with the ST_Flip anchor (ST_Flip_Event != 0):
      LONG   1   ADX_Rising:==0 ->8-> D2D_DirStep:==-1
      LONG 182   Sqz_Val:hi ->13-> Micro_OrderFlowDelta:lo
  A build reading v1 literally drops or mis-parses one of these, and the F1
  path needs an anchor the F0 path does not supply.

  MANDATORY FOURTH TERM ON EVERY SIGNAL, BOTH FAMILIES:
      D2D_Trend_Dir == trade direction

STEP 3 — DEPTH. THIS IS NOT v1's DEFINITION.

  DEPTH IS THE NUMBER OF SIGNALS ADMITTED IN THE BATCH AT THAT BAR IN THAT
  DIRECTION. It is decided in STEP 4, before any position opens, and it is
  knowable at bar close.

  It is NOT the count of signals that qualify (a different, worse system).
  It is NOT v1's executed depth, which was only knowable AFTER a bar's
  admissions finished and so could not be evaluated at decision time.

STEP 4 — ADMISSION (FLOORED). THE BAR IS DECIDED AS ONE BATCH.

  At bar close, compute once:
      live = active trades that are NOT be_nudged
      free = MAX_POSITIONS - live                              (21 - live)

  Then for each direction d in (LONG, SHORT):
      A = signals of direction d qualifying at this bar AND not already
          holding a position
      if len(A) == 0            -> nothing this direction
      if free < 3               -> admit nothing this direction
      n = min(len(A), free)
      if n < 3                  -> admit nothing this direction
      evaluate STEP 5 and STEP 6 at depth n
      if they pass              -> ADMIT ALL n; free = free - n
      else                      -> admit nothing this direction

  NO POSITION IS EVER OPENED AND THEN REMOVED. There is no post-hoc filter.
  Backtest equals live, exactly.

  ADMISSION ORDER IS LONG THEN SHORT — and it is INERT. SHORT-then-LONG and a
  depth-first order (deeper direction served first) both produce results
  IDENTICAL TO THE CENT. Free slots are never scarce enough for the two
  directions to compete. Implement either; it does not matter. See 8.3.

STEP 5 — GLOBAL GATE
  ATR_1M >= 20 at the entry bar. Raw ATR, not a percentile.

STEP 6 — PER-TIER GATE STACK.  tier = min(depth, 5).

  tier   LONG                                            SHORT
  ----   ------------------------------------------      ------------------
  1      unreachable (floor is 3)                        unreachable
  2      unreachable (floor is 3)                        unreachable
  3      Micro_Hurst > p90                               Micro_Hurst > p90
  4      Micro_FailedBreak > p20 AND AT_Slope_ST > p90   FREE (measured, 5.3)
  5+     Micro_FailedBreak > p20                         FREE (measured, 5.3)

  All thresholds are mechanism-D adaptive percentiles at the entry bar.
  A BUILD MUST NOT CARRY THE TIER-1 AND TIER-2 CELLS. They are unreachable
  under a floor of 3 and are not part of this system.

STEP 7 — THE JAR
  live_lots = count of active trades that are NOT be_nudged. Cap 21.
  THE JAR COUNTS AT-RISK POSITIONS, NOT LOTS AND NOT OPEN POSITIONS. A trade
  past its break-even nudge cannot lose and frees its slot. This is why peak
  OPEN reaches 42 while peak AT-RISK is exactly 21.

STEP 8 — CONVICTION SIZING
  Multiplies lots. Never blocks. ASSIGNMENTS, not products, so max is 2.0:
      Micro_Hurst > p90 on longs                          x2.0
      D2D agreement (D2D_Signal == dir AND ADX >= 30
         AND Micro_Hurst >= p30), both directions         x2.0
      recentfb_sizing                                     DISABLED — see 3.2

STEP 9 — TRADE MANAGEMENT
  momentum      = Micro_LogReturn * direction >= 0.00012
  raw_risk      = ATR_1M * (4.0 if momentum else 2.0)
  initial_risk  = min(raw_risk, 150.0)      <- 150 POINTS. NOT DOLLARS.
  stop          = entry -/+ initial_risk
  base_risk     = min(ATR_1M * 2.0, 150.0)
  step_size     = 0.30 * base_risk
  be_trigger    = 1.00 * step_size
  be_lock_dist  = 1.00 * be_trigger
  lag           = 3 if momentum else 2
  SPREAD        = 3.0 points, charged on every trade
  Entry price is the bar CLOSE, so every trade opened on the same bar in the
  same direction has an IDENTICAL entry, stop, lag and conviction multiplier.

8.4  THE CEILING, AND THE ONE ASSUMPTION IT RESTS ON
  ceiling = MAX_POSITIONS x MAX_RISK x max conviction x lot
          = 21 x 150 x 2.0 x lot     ($1,890 at 0.30 = 37.8% of daily)

  It counts AT-RISK positions only. Peak OPEN reaches 42 while peak AT-RISK is
  exactly 21, and the difference is legitimate ONLY BECAUSE BREAK-EVEN-LOCKED
  POSITIONS CANNOT LOSE (6.3, from source and from measurement).

  IT IS TRUE OF THE SIMULATOR BY CONSTRUCTION. IT IS TRUE OF LIVE ONLY TO THE
  EXTENT FILLS ARE CLEAN. The engine fills at current_sl EXACTLY whenever the
  bar touches it. There is no slippage model and no gap model anywhere in the
  exit path. A gap through the lock fills BELOW it and a locked position would
  then lose. This frame cannot measure how far.
    - the ceiling is EXACT under the simulator's fill assumption
    - the ceiling is a LOWER BOUND live
    - every figure in 6.4 inherits the same assumption
    - the true live ceiling is (at-risk x MAX_RISK x conviction x lot) +
      (locked x gap-through-lock), and the second term is UNMEASURED
  THIS IS WHAT THE DEMO IS FOR.

8.5  THE SOLO/DUAL PATH WAS TESTED AND REJECTED
  A separately-gated d1/d2 admission path was built and swept — 10 free
  relocations at SHORT d1+d2, guarded so it fires only when the market offered
  1-2 signals and nothing was chopped.
  IT FAILS ON ITS OWN METRIC. Best exchange rate 0.28 episodes per loss event
  added, against 0.15 for simply enlarging the book — under 2x, one trial, no
  null. And the two genuinely strict gates (pass rates 0.489% and 0.684%)
  REACH ZERO NEW EPISODES while costing 2 and 13 loss events.
  STRICTNESS AND COVERAGE ARE ANTI-CORRELATED AND THE RANKING IS THE SAME
  ORDER. Any condition rare enough to be trusted alone will almost never be
  present on one of the two bars of a 2-bar episode. This is arithmetic, not a
  tuning failure.

11. WHAT THIS DOCUMENT DOES NOT CLAIM

It does not claim this composition is optimal — 7.3 shows it is not
distinguishable from 59 alternatives out-of-sample.
It does not claim the composition has been tested forward. It has not.
It does not claim 43 loss events support a confidence interval on anything
day-keyed; those rest on 36 days.

reconstruction with a stated, arguable convention.
It does not claim a second implementation has reproduced any of this.
It does not claim the six LONG orphans are justified by a statistic. They are
justified by a measured contribution and labelled as such in 4.2.

WHAT IT DOES CLAIM: the signal layer was selected for 4-of-6-month consistency
and survived an independent rebuild at 84.6%; the one true holdout available is
the strongest month in the book; seven of seven months and twenty-six of
twenty-six weeks are positive; and across sixty complete compositions and three
walk-forward windows every single one is profitable.

THE SIGNALS CARRY IT. THE COMPOSITION IS A PREFERENCE.
