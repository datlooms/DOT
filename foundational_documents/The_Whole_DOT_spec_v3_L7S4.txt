WHY THIS DOCUMENT EXISTS

  CONCURRENCE STACKING IS THE PERSISTING ATTRIBUTE OF THESE SIGNALS. It is the
  one thing that has survived every rebuild, every re-pricing, every admission
  change and every gate re-derivation. This document is the cleanest available
  demonstration of it: take the deployed book, change ONE number, and the loss
  population falls from 42 events to 16 while the profit factor rises from 14.53
  to 25.91.

  A new analyst building the selection process into discovery should read
  section 6 first. THE LADDER IS THE POINT. Everything else here is context.

0.3  A DEFECT FOUND IN adm_engine.py WHILE DOING THIS — REPORT IT

  adm_engine.py L312 reads:

      if ADMISSION_RULE != 'CURRENT':

  and the module default at L43 is ADMISSION_RULE = 'CURRENT'. THE ENTIRE
  FLOORED ADMISSION BLOCK IS SKIPPED UNLESS THE CALLER SETS IT EXPLICITLY. A
  first control run against this fork produced 18,416 trades instead of 5,776
  and did not error, did not warn, and responded identically to every floor
  value passed.

  SHAPE: THE SAME PERMISSIVE-FALLBACK CLASS AS D4. A missing setting defaulted
  to the permitted, looser behaviour. The fork is named for FLOORED admission
  and does not perform it by default.

  THE FULL INVOCATION A SECOND PARTY MUST USE:

      AE.ADMISSION_RULE = 'FLOORED'
      AE.ADM_FLOOR      = {1: <long_floor>, -1: <short_floor>}
      AE.MAX_POSITIONS  = <cap>
      AE.ADM_ORDER      = (1, -1)
      AE.ADM_GATES      = {'ATR': df['ATR_1M'].values, 'atr_min': 20.0}
      AE.ADM_TIERGATES  = {(+1, 3): [HU90], (+1, 4): [FB20, ATS90],
                           (+1, 5): [FB20],
                           (-1, 3): [HU90], (-1, 4): [], (-1, 5): []}
      conviction built with recentfb = False

  ADM_TIERGATES KEYS ARE INTEGER DIRECTIONS, NOT STRINGS. The signals CSV must
  also be parsed into feat_1..3 / thresh_1..3; build_signal_masks reads those
  columns and whole_dot_signals.csv ships only signal_def.

2. THE RULE, IN EXECUTION ORDER
================================================================================

Identical to spec v3 section 2 in every step EXCEPT STEP 6. Only the floor
differs. Reproduced here so this document is self-contained.

STEP 1 — BAR ELIGIBILITY
    post-warmup (bar >= warmup_floor); ADX_Value >= 15; Volume > 50;
    Friday cutoff.

STEP 2 — SIGNAL QUALIFICATION
    F0 triple-convergence: three conditions of the form V:hi / V:lo / V:==N,
    ANDed, thresholds from dots_thresholds mechanism D (rolling-2500 causal
    percentile, day-refreshed). MANDATORY FOURTH TERM: D2D_Trend_Dir == the
    trade direction. All 297 signals are F0 triples — one grammar, no family
    split.

STEP 3 — DEPTH
    Depth is the count ADMITTED IN THE FLOORED BATCH, knowable at decision
    time. NOT qualification depth, and NOT the 249-condition count.

STEP 4 — GLOBAL GATE
    ATR_1M >= 20 (raw, at the entry bar).

STEP 5 — TIER GATES
    tier = min(depth, 5). See section 5 — MOST OF THE STACK IS UNREACHABLE AT
    THIS FLOOR AND THAT IS THE HEADLINE FINDING OF THIS DOCUMENT.

STEP 6 — THE FLOOR  *** THE ONLY DIFFERENCE FROM v3 ***
    LONG  admit only if the FLOORED batch size >= 7
    SHORT admit only if the FLOORED batch size >= 4
    (v3 deployed: 3 and 3)

STEP 7 — FLOORED ADMISSION
    Batch the bar: n = min(admissible, free). Refuse the batch entirely if
    n < floor. No trade is opened and later deleted.

STEP 8 — THE JAR
    live_lots counts ONLY positions that are not be_nudged. A position at
    break-even leaves the jar and frees its slot.

STEP 9 — CONVICTION SIZING
    Hurst x2.0, D2D-agree x2.0, D2D gap. recentfb_sizing FALSE.

STEP 10 — TRADE MANAGEMENT
    RISK_MULT 2.0 / MOMENTUM_SL_MULT 4.0 / MAX_RISK 150 POINTS /
    LOCK_FRAC 1.0 / STEP_PCT 0.30 / LAG_BASE 2 / LAG_MOMENTUM 3 /
    momentum condition (Micro_LogReturn x direction) >= 0.00012.

                          IS NOT A CLIFF — it is a point on a monotone curve,
                          selected here to demonstrate the ladder, not because
                          a measurement identified it.

  max_positions      21   INHERITED FROM v3, WHERE IT IS THE LAST SAFE VALUE
                          BEFORE A WORST-DAY CLIFF. AT 7/4 THERE IS NO CLIFF
                          (section 7). 21 is carried so the two documents are
                          comparable, NOT because it is optimal here.

THE FLOORS IN THIS DOCUMENT ARE A DEMONSTRATION, NOT A DERIVATION. Anyone
adopting them must derive them.

  establish that price did not reach the stop on that bar. Positions past the BE
  nudge are floored at the lock level. RESIDUAL CONSERVATISM: all open positions
  are assumed to reach their worst moment simultaneously. True drawdowns are
  shallower by an unmeasured amount.

================================================================================

  TWO REASONS, BOTH THE OPERATOR'S, BOTH MEASURED.

  10.1  PARTICIPATION. 110 days of 132 against 120. The 7/4 book is silent on
        22 trading days — one day in six. A book that goes quiet is hard to run,
        hard to trust and hard to test. It also earns 20% less in absolute terms
        despite the better ratios, because it trades 30% less.

  10.2  AUDITABILITY, AND THIS IS THE BINDING REASON.

        EVERY RATIO IN THIS DOCUMENT RESTS ON SIXTEEN BAD BARS IN SIX MONTHS.
        The 97.40% win rate, the 25.91 profit factor, the four losing days, the
        zero losing weeks — all sixteen events. Day-keyed figures rest on
        fourteen.

        AT SIXTEEN EVENTS YOU CANNOT DISTINGUISH A REAL PROBLEM FROM NOISE
        INSIDE A YEAR OF LIVE TRADING. Three bad bars in a month is either
        variance or the end of the edge, and nothing in the data tells you
        which. The deployed book's 42 events on 35 days is not a comfortable
        sample either, but it is more than twice the evidence for 27% more
        money and 10 more trading days.

        FEWER LOSSES IS FEWER EVENTS TO MEASURE WITH. THEY ARE THE SAME NUMBER.
        A configuration that loses less is not automatically safer to run; it is
        harder to verify, and verification is what protects the account over a
        year.

  10.3  AND THE GATE PROBLEM. Section 5: the floor makes the system's only
        confirmed gate unreachable. Adopting 7/4 means either running with one
        real gate or re-deriving the stack at LONG d7+ and SHORT d4+ — and gate
        derivation at this floor would face 16 events across five reachable
        cells, roughly 3 events per cell. THAT IS NOT ENOUGH TO FIT ANYTHING.

11. WHAT THIS DOCUMENT DOES NOT CLAIM
================================================================================

It does not claim 7 and 4 are derived. They are not (section 3). v3's 3 and 3
are derived from a measured cliff; these are a demonstration point.
It does not claim the gate stack is appropriate at this floor. Most of it is
unreachable and the best-supported gate cannot fire (section 5).
It does not claim MAX_POSITIONS 21 is optimal here. There is no cliff at this
floor and the curve is still rising uncapped (section 7).
It does not claim 16 loss events support a confidence interval on anything.
Day-keyed figures rest on 14.
It does not claim the intraday figures are engine output. They are a
reconstruction with a stated convention (section 8.3).
It does not claim the out-of-sample windows test the selection process. They
test this book on a field built with full-sample statistics.
It does not claim June is understood. It is not.

WHAT IT DOES CLAIM, AND IT IS THE ONLY REASON THE DOCUMENT EXISTS:

  THE SAME 297 SIGNALS, THE SAME ENGINE, THE SAME GATES AND THE SAME CAP —
  CHANGING ONLY THE DEPTH FLOOR FROM 3/3 TO 7/4 — TAKE THE LOSS POPULATION FROM
  42 EVENTS TO 16, THE PROFIT FACTOR FROM 14.53 TO 25.91, AND THE DURABILITY
  MARGIN FROM 33.07 TO 38.25 POINTS. LONG DEPTH 13 AND ABOVE IS 978 TRADES WITH
  ZERO LOSSES.

  CONCURRENCE STACKING IS THE PERSISTING ATTRIBUTE OF THESE SIGNALS. THAT IS
  WHAT THE SELECTION PROCESS HAS TO PRESERVE.
