 2. THE RULE, IN EVALUATION ORDER
================================================================================

  Each step read from portfolio_simulation_engine.py at the ratified sha. The
  order below is the order the engine executes.

  STEP 1 - BAR ELIGIBILITY (engine, entry block)
      post-warmup: bar >= DOTS_INITBARS
      ADX_Value >= 15
      Volume > 50
      Friday cutoff: not (EST_DayOfWeek==5 and (hour>16 or (hour==16 and min>=45)))

  STEP 2 - SIGNAL QUALIFICATION
      F0 triple-convergence: three conditions of the form V:hi, V:lo or V:==N,
      ANDed. Thresholds from dots_thresholds.py mechanism D (rolling-2500 causal
      percentile, day-refreshed) or STRUCTURAL constants.
      MANDATORY FOURTH TERM: D2D_Trend_Dir == trade direction.
      (One of the 299 is an F1 sequential pair, A ->k-> B, scored by
      sequential_temporal.pair_mask. It is listed in section 4.)

  STEP 3 - CONCURRENCE DEPTH
      Per (bar, direction): the count of DISTINCT BOOK SIGNALS qualifying on that
      bar in that direction.

      THIS IS NOT THE 249-CONDITION COUNT. That quantity has median 27 and never
      falls below 8, and conflating the two caused a full pipeline rebuild earlier
      in this project. Signal-count depth is what every rule below refers to.

  STEP 4 - GLOBAL GATE
      ATR_1M >= 20

  STEP 5 - PER-TIER GATE STACK (see section 3 for thresholds, section 5 for which
      gates are reachable)

  STEP 6 - DEPTH FLOOR
      LONG:  admit only if depth >= 3
      SHORT: admit only if depth >= 3

  STEP 7 - JAR
      live_lots = count of active trades that are NOT be_nudged
      if live_lots >= MAX_POSITIONS: refuse the entry

      THE JAR COUNTS AT-RISK POSITIONS ONLY. A trade that reaches break-even has
      its stop at entry + lock and cannot lose; it leaves the jar and its slot
      frees. This is why peak OPEN positions (39) far exceeds peak AT-RISK (12).

  STEP 8 - CONVICTION SIZING (multiplies lots, never blocks)
      Micro_Hurst > p90 on longs        x2.0
      recent FailedBreak within 5 bars  x1.25
      D2D agreement                     x2.0

  STEP 9 - TRADE MANAGEMENT (see section 3)
      momentum classification, stop placement, BE nudge, tier trail, exit.

    rarity condition (Bar_Range > p95) and d4 an 80th-percentile one

    5 -> 4. ALL THREE OUT-OF-SAMPLE WINDOWS IMPROVED.

================================================================================
 7. THE RISK SECTION
================================================================================

  THE BAR IS THE RISK UNIT, NOT THE TRADE.

  201 losses collapse to 47 DISTINCT BAR-EVENTS. When a bar goes against the book,
  every signal that fired on it loses together - clusters run 3 to 9 trades. Every
  loss rate, confidence interval and profit factor in this document rests on 47
  events in six months, NOT on 201 independent observations. A Wilson interval on
  201/5,107 is wrong by construction.

  ALL 47 EVENTS ARE SL EXITS. No BE or LF exit has ever lost. The book has exactly
  one failure mode.

    many BE-locked positions are open. Observed peak at-risk is exactly 12

  (grep-confirmed), so every intraday figure here is a reconstruction. Each open

  position cannot be marked below a level at which it would already have closed,
  and the engine's own exit decisions establish that price did not reach the stop
  on that bar, so no intrabar ordering assumption is required. Positions past the

  positions are assumed to reach their worst moment simultaneously. TRUE
  DRAWDOWNS ARE SHALLOWER BY AN UNMEASURED AMOUNT.

 8. THE ALTERNATIVE HELD IN RESERVE - "+M1+M3"
================================================================================

  TWO FURTHER GATE RELOCATIONS, MEASURED AND NOT ADOPTED.

    M1  SHORT d3 <- the SHORT solo stacked pair (Bar_Range < p95 AND
        FailedBreak < p10). MECHANISM: Option B gated its two shallowest short
        tiers and left triple+ free. At a floor of 3, d3 IS the shallowest short
        tier traded, so the shallow-short gate moves to it. THE STACKED FORM IS
        NOT OPTIONAL - either condition alone gives roughly half the profit factor.

    M3  LONG d5-6 <- AT_Slope_ST > p90 (the LONG dual gate). MECHANISM: d5+ carries
        VolOfVol > p20, an 80th-percentile weak gate - the same shape as the
        FailedBreak > p20 that the adopted relocation fixed.

  MEASURED, cap 12:

                        adopted        +M1+M3      delta
    trades               5,107          3,511      -31%
    days traded            123            120        -3
    LOSS EVENTS             47             20       -27
    losing-bar rate      4.38%          3.08%
    PF                   13.02          20.01
    losing days              4              1        -3
    net               $255,273       $210,946   -$44,327
    margin pts           30.86          32.37
    OOS split 0/1/2   1.75/6.92/6.06  1.72/3.83/3.50

  IT HALVES BOTH WEAK OUT-OF-SAMPLE WINDOWS. That is the strongest evidence for
  it and it is why it is not discarded.

  WHY IT IS NOT ADOPTED. 20 loss events is the floor of what can be audited. At 20
  events a single bad week moves the loss rate by a fifth, the one-losing-day claim
  rests on one observation, and deterioration cannot be distinguished from noise
  for months. FEWER LOSSES IS FEWER EVENTS TO MEASURE WITH - THEY ARE THE SAME
  NUMBER.

  THIS IS THE UPGRADE PATH. The relocations cost nothing to apply later. Move to
  +M1+M3 once live trading has produced enough loss events to tell the two
  configurations apart. The evidence to choose is not free to acquire; the change
  itself is.

  TRIAL COUNT FOR THE RELOCATION WORK: 3 mechanism-stated moves, 5 combinations
  evaluated, 1 adopted. Not priced against a null. What supports them is that
  every surviving move improved at least two of three out-of-sample windows.

 10. WHAT IS NOT PROVEN - DO NOT SOFTEN THIS SECTION
================================================================================

  10.1  NOTHING IS OUT-OF-SAMPLE. Every headline figure is in-sample on one
        six-month frame. The three "walk-forward windows" come from a book
        selected on the full sample.

  10.2  THE PROCESS IS NOT WALKED FORWARD. Only one of the four union sources
        (S0-120) and none of the underlying field can be re-derived inside a
        training segment. Doing it properly needs S3 discovery re-executed per
        segment - a pipeline run, not an analysis.

  10.3  THE FIELD CARRIES LOOK-AHEAD. Every signal cleared agg_pf >= 2.0 and
        folds_plus >= 4 measured over the WHOLE six months. Recomputed on training
        data only, ONLY 42% OF THE 299 WOULD HAVE BEEN IN THE FIELD ON SPLIT 0,
        rising to 78% and 86% by splits 1 and 2. The contamination shrinks as the
        window lengthens, which is what durable signals would produce - but the
        early-window result is not trustworthy.

  10.4  SPLIT 1 IS THE WEAK WINDOW. May-June, 6.92% loss rate against 1.75% and
        6.06% in the other two. It has been the soft window in EVERY configuration
        tested across this entire study and no explanation survived: not ATR, not
        the stop cap, not regime rotation, not path composition, not signal
        composition. The regime-0 within-regime deterioration (1.08% -> 8.38%,
        March to June) is real, bounded on five sides, and UNEXPLAINED.

  10.5  THE 47-EVENT BASE BOUNDS EVERY RISK CLAIM. Four losing days is four
        observations. Zero losing weeks in 26 is a statement about 47 events.

  10.6  THE SELECTION BURDEN IS UNPRICEABLE. Roughly 90 book-level configurations
        were scored across this study against a target defined after seeing the
        incumbent's numbers. That cannot be priced retrospectively. What mitigates
        it: a random-book null showed the surrounding configuration space performs
        (18 of 30 cells passed both acceptance criteria; top-10 net spread 1.45x),
        so the candidate sits on a plateau rather than a spike.

  10.7  THE ENGINE HAS NO FLOATING-EQUITY TRACKING. Every intraday figure is a
        reconstruction (method in section 7). FTMO liquidates on equity including
        open positions, so this is the figure that decides deployability and it is
        the one the simulator does not produce.

  10.8  CAP 8 IS THE WORST SETTING IN EVERY CONFIGURATION TESTED, on three
        separate books, and the reason is unknown. The cap cannot be chosen by
        interpolation between measured points.

 11. THE FINDINGS THIS CONFIGURATION RESTS ON
================================================================================

  Three structural facts, none of them fitted, each measured before the books that
  use them existed:

  11.1  THE UNION. Four selection objectives with four different error modes.
        S0-120 overlaps every prior selection by 1-8% and contributes 111 unique
        signals. Fusing them broke a $98K net ceiling that had held across four
        very differently-constructed 120-signal books.

  11.2  DEPTH CARRIES THE LOSSES AT THE SHALLOW END. 39 of Option B's 42 losses
        sat at depth <= 3, measured before any union book was built. In this book
        depth 7+ has ZERO losses on 1,635 trades.

  11.3  THE PER-DIRECTION PRINCIPLE. LONG d4 at 10.50% against SHORT d4 at 0.78%.
        Every absolute threshold in this pipeline has been wrong in the same
        direction - the 30-trade floor, the folds_plus bar, the confirmer offset,
        the depth floor - and per-direction was the correction every time.

  AND ONE PROPERTY OF DEPTH THAT MUST BE STATED PRECISELY, because the mantra
  overstates it in one direction:

    DEEP CONCURRENCE IS A RELIABLE INDICATOR THAT A BAR SITS IN A LARGE MOVE -
    33.4% of traded bars are top-decile 30-bar moves against a 10% baseline, a
    3.3x enrichment - AND A VERY POOR DETECTOR OF LARGE MOVES, at 0.75% recall.
    "Depth is a size measurement" is TRUE AS PRECISION and FALSE AS RECALL.

  And the terrain measurement that justifies size on deep bars, with its limit:
  bars carrying 12+ lots show median forward displacement of 49.3 points over 30
  bars (0.99 ATR units), the highest ATR, ADX, volume and efficiency ratio of any
  band, 100% win rate and $1,308 per bar - and that band is 87 BARS. The 8-12 lot
  bands are the opposite: median forward displacement 7.5 and 0.0 points, the
  lowest win rate in the book. DEPTH ONLY BECOMES AN EVENT-SIZE DETECTOR ABOVE 12
  LOTS, and the 87-bar basis was priced at the 100th percentile of 5,000 random
  87-bar subsets.
